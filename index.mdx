Welcome to the official documentation for the **Sasini Mobile Application**. This platform bridges the gap between traditional agricultural operations and cutting-edge technology, unifying over 30,000 farmers, supply chain administrators, and automated backend APIs into a single, seamless, and highly secure environment.

<Note>
  **Current Scope:** While the app's architecture dynamically supports multiple commodities (Coffee, Macadamia, Avocado), this documentation currently focuses on our live, active deployment for **Tea Farmers**.
</Note>

---

## Platform Capabilities by Role

The Sasini ecosystem operates on a strict, role-based architecture. Choose your perspective below to see exactly how the platform empowers each stakeholder.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="👨‍🌾 The Farmer App">
    **The Pocket Command Center**
    A robust, fully localized mobile interface (Swahili, French, German, English) designed to work seamlessly even in remote highland regions.

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion icon="sack-dollar" title="Produce & Financials">
        * **Delivery Tracking:** Real-time visibility into weighbridge deliveries with advanced date filtering and live total-yield statistics.
        * **Payment Overview:** Instant access to payment histories, breakdowns, and downloadable digital receipts.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion icon="tractor" title="Farm Management & Logistics">
        * **Input Ordering:** Browse, order, and track the status of genuine seeds, fertilizers, and farming tools.
        * **Pickup Requests:** Dispatch Sasini logistics fleets directly to your farm's exact GPS coordinates.
        * **Pest Reports:** Instantly report localized outbreaks with geolocated images to alert factory agronomy teams.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion icon="microchip" title="AI, Insights & Weather">
        * **Sasini AI Assistant:** A highly-tuned agricultural chatbot powered by **Google Gemini** for instant agronomy advice.
        * **Live Weather:** GPS-native forecasting and severe weather alerts.
        * **News & Markets:** Live updates on global commodity auction prices and official factory announcements.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion icon="user-gear" title="Account & Externals">
        * **Complete Control:** Multi-factor authentication, profile management, and zero-trust account sovereignty.
        * **External Hubs:** Quick SSO links to the Sasini Retail Store and the Farmer Training Center.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="🏢 Admin Web Console">
    **The Factory Nerve Center**
    Sasini administrators and extension officers utilize a centralized, high-security web dashboard to govern the entire ecosystem.

    * **Logistics & Fleet:** Monitor incoming pickup requests, approve farm input orders, and triage active pest reports.
    * **Broadcast Communications:** Publish breaking news, update live market prices, and push out critical weather or drought alerts.
    * **User Governance:** Verify KYC parameters for new farmer registrations, process account lifecycle requests, and resolve support tickets.
    * **Targeted Push:** Dispatch personalized, highly specific push notifications to individual farmers or entire geographic regions.
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="💻 Developer Architecture">
    **The Technical Backbone**

    

    The application relies on a scalable, zero-trust microservice architecture connecting internal factory ERPs with global tech providers.

    * **Google Cloud Integrations:** Direct, low-latency server-to-server connections.
    * **LLM Integration:** Utilizing **Google Gemini** models for the core AI Assistant.
    * **Dynamic Data:** Integrating Google Search algorithms and MSN Weather APIs for real-time localization.
    * **Zero-Trust Security:** End-to-end payload encryption, JWT session management, rigorous password hashing, and immutable Digital IDs.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
